NFL award chances

Probability each player or coach wins the season's major awards.

Each award is a choice model fitted to the AP winners from 2010–2025. Every candidate's season is projected from stats to date, with their team's wins taken from the season simulation, and the winner is picked in every simulated season. The badge shows how the same Week 4 forecast did in 2018–2025.

Built from nflverse public data (play-by-play, schedules, player stats, rosters, injury reports, FTN and Pro Football Reference charting). An independent model. Probabilities only, not betting advice.
