Methodology

What is in the first NBA model, what is not, and how to interpret its probabilities.

Team strength

  • Every team game is converted to points per estimated possession.
  • A regularized model separates team offense from opponent defense across the latest four seasons.
  • Older seasons receive less weight. Once the current season begins, its games receive extra weight so live performance gradually replaces the preseason prior.
  • Current rosters are compared with last season using a deliberately conservative, minutes-weighted box-score impact estimate.

Season simulation

  • Played games are locked in and all remaining regular-season games are simulated from team strength, home court and game-level variance.
  • Before NBA Cup flex matchups are published, balanced placeholder games complete every team to 82 games; official fixtures replace them automatically.
  • Each simulation also draws a season-long uncertainty term for every team.
  • The NBA play-in format is simulated for seeds 7–10, followed by the fixed conference bracket and best-of-seven series.
  • Home court in a playoff series goes to the better simulated regular-season record, with model strength breaking exact ties.

Inputs and stats

  • Historical team and player box scores, schedules and rosters come from public ESPN-derived SportsDataverse releases.
  • The Stats page includes up to ten seasons of team and player box-score data, with regular season and playoffs separated.
  • It derives estimated possessions, pace, offensive/defensive rating, net rating, effective field-goal percentage, true-shooting percentage, three-point rate, free-throw rate and player usage from box-score totals.
  • These are descriptive stats, not opponent-adjusted projections. The model uses no sportsbook odds or market prices.
  • Four seasons feed the live rating.

Known limitations

  • The current player adjustment is box-score based, not possession-level RAPM.
  • Rookies, coaching changes, role changes and recovery from injuries need explicit analyst overrides.
  • Standings ties use simulated point differential and rating after wins, rather than every official multi-team tiebreak procedure.
  • Version 0.1 has not yet demonstrated an edge against futures markets. Probabilities are decision support, not recommendations.
