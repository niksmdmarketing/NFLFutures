What drives the projection

  • Possession-based offense and defense fitted across the latest four seasons, with recent results weighted most heavily.
  • Current rosters are compared with last season using a conservative box-score player impact estimate. The adjustment is deliberately shrunk.
  • Every remaining regular-season game, the play-in and each best-of-seven playoff series are simulated with home court and team uncertainty.

Read with care

  • The model does not use bookmaker prices, so it is independent but not automatically better than the market.
  • Rookies, coaching changes and injuries are not fully valued yet. Those matter most before the season starts.
  • The next version will add a historical preseason backtest and richer possession/player features.
