      Each cell shows the offense rank, then the defense rank it faces (1 is best, 32 is worst), as they stood going into that week. Select a matchup to open its rank trend.

      How the ranks are built

      • Every regular-season run and pass play from this season, plus last season at a lower weight so early-season noise settles. Its influence fades as new games come in.
      • Each play is scored by success rate and EPA. The two are blended 65/35, leaning on success rate because it is steadier from week to week.
      • Offense and defense strength are estimated together in one regression, so a team is credited for the opponents it faced and small samples are pulled toward average.
      • Teams are ranked 1 to 32 in each category. The edge is the gap between the offense rank and the defense rank it faces.

      The five categories

      • Overall: every run and pass play.
      • Passing: dropbacks, with sacks and scrambles counted.
      • Rushing: designed runs only.
      • Pressure: pressures allowed or generated per dropback, from Pro Football Reference charting via nflverse, with last season blended in early on. Not opponent-adjusted.
      • Explosives: share of plays gaining 15+ yards passing or 10+ rushing.

      Edge labels: Even when the rank gap is 7 or less, Edge for 8 to 14, Big edge for 15 or more.
