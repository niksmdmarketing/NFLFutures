# NHL playoff series calibration: is the live series probability calibrated? Rule fixed before any logging or scoring (8 Nov 2026, Sydney)

Status: **final before building. Nothing in this rule has been run.** Live NHL futures stay unchanged whatever the result; any change needs its own dated rule and a fresh look.

## Why
research/NHL_PO_SCALE_RULE.md (Amendments 1-6): the nine-season Cup slope (live 1.591, 90% [1.07, 2.38]) cannot separate a series-variance defect from a regular-season or prior defect, because every futures market is a compound (P(make playoffs) x P(win round 1) x ...). The only quantity that isolates the series channel is P(A beats B | A and B meet). About 15 realised series a season, 135 over the nine seasons.

## Estimand (written down so it cannot drift)
For each realised playoff series s (home-ice team h, opponent o): q_s = the model's P(h wins the series | h and o meet), computed at the end of the regular season (all 82 games played, ratings and tau draws as the live path would use them, no knowledge of any playoff result), averaged over the simulated tau draws. Outcome y_s = 1 if h won. Scoring is conditional on the two teams meeting. It says nothing about seeding, bracket or who reaches which round, and no such claim may be made from this data.

## Stage 1: diagnostic of the live setting (no knob, no tuning)
- Live configuration: canonical string of NHL_GOALIE_CANONICAL_RULE.md, TAU_PRE live, po_gap_only off. Baseline is rebuilt in-session, never read from a stored file.
- Statistic: logistic calibration slope of y_s on logit(q_s), intercept free, season-clustered wild bootstrap 90% interval (Rademacher weights, 9 clusters, 10,000 draws, seed 20261108). Also reported: mean log loss and Brier of q_s, and the slope by round (1, 2, 3, final).
- Clustering: a pre-registered band for the within-season correlation, rho in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}. The finding stands only if it holds at rho 0.20. The minimum detectable slope movement is computed after logging and before scoring from the logged q_s spread (sd of logit q_s, a prediction-only quantity) and the band, and written into this file as a dated amendment. Outcomes are not used to set it.
- Outcomes: **Calibrated** if the interval contains 1. **Defect** if the interval excludes 1 at rho 0.20. **Insufficient** if the interval contains 1 and the half-width exceeds the minimum detectable movement. No other outcome.

## Stage 2: only if Stage 1 says Defect
A separate dated rule names one knob setting in advance, with the live setting reproduced bit for bit as the assertion (both knobs at the live schedule), and states its own minimum detectable movement. No grid on these seasons. If Stage 1 says Calibrated, the Cup-slope flatness is not in the series channel and the po_scale question is closed for this data.

## Logging requirement
simulate() gains an optional list of (home, away) pairs and returns the mean series-win probability for each pair computed analytically from the per-simulation game probabilities (no extra random draws, so every existing output is unchanged; tested against the sampled frequency). The replay adds one end-of-regular-season checkpoint per season and writes series_<season>.csv: season, round, home, away, q, outcome, home-ice flag, plus knobs.json beside it. Realised pairs come from games_<season>p.json.

## Disclosed exposure
Live PO_FADE and TAU_PRE were chosen on the same seasons for Cup and title log loss. Series outcomes were not used. Seasons 2015-2018 and 2021-2025 as in the canonical rule.

## Amendment 1 (8 Nov 2026, reviewer round 9 checked; recorded before any logging or run; nothing above edited)
**Found while checking the reviewer's points (not raised by the reviewer): the original estimand tests the wrong regime.** The live playoff gap is stretched by po_scale = 1 + PO_FADE x max(0, 1 - games/82): 4x at game 0, 1x at game 82 (replay_nhl.py:345). The Cup-slope question is a preseason question, where the gap and the tau draw are both stretched 4x. A series probability taken at the end of the regular season has po_scale 1, where po_gap_only on and off are identical, so it cannot test what the po_scale work asked. Corrected: P(home wins | meet) for each realised pair is logged at **every replay checkpoint** (preseason week 0, first quarter, mid-season, end of regular season), because it is defined for any pair at any date. **Primary checkpoint: preseason (week 0); second: first quarter.** The end-of-season checkpoint is reported as the live-regime check. The model does not update ratings during the playoffs, so "at series start" equals the end-of-season rating for every round.
1. **Companion slopes (reviewer Hole 1, accepted).** Reported beside the series slope at the same checkpoints, from the same in-session baseline: playoff-qualification slope, division slope, and the points slope (linear regression of actual season points on pts_mean, which isolates rating spread from series noise). "Regular-season channel consistent with calibrated" means the 90% interval of each of those three contains 1 at that checkpoint. Decision: companions consistent with 1 and series interval excluding 1 -> series-channel defect, Stage 2 may be proposed. Series interval excluding 1 in the same direction as a companion that also excludes 1 -> inherited from the ratings, Stage 2 is not authorised. Note for the record: the reviewer's premise that every live slope sits above 1 is not what the nine-season live arm shows (preseason: division 0.91, round 2 0.89, playoff 1.16 [0.94, 1.42]; round 3 1.51, finals 1.92, Cup 1.59). The late-round markets sit above 1 and the regular-season ones do not, which is the pattern this test is built to resolve, not an assumption of it.
2. **No reuse of the same series (Hole 2, accepted).** Stage 2 names its single setting from the 2015-2018 series only (or from reasoning without outcomes), is scored on 2021-2025, and any adoption is forward-only (shadow from 2026-27). Stage 1 screens on all nine seasons and does not pick the setting. Cost stated: the early block is about 60 series, so a Stage 2 split is low power and "insufficient" is the expected honest result.
3. **Clustering band (Hole 3, accepted in part).** rho band becomes {0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40}; the minimum detectable movement is quoted at rho 0.20 and 0.40 with the rho beside each number, and an Insufficient call is made against the rho 0.40 figure. Design effect 1 + (m - 1) rho with m about 15: 3.8 at 0.20 (n_eff about 36), 6.6 at 0.40 (n_eff about 20), a 32% wider interval. Not accepted: estimating rho from a disjoint window (no cache exists for seasons before 2015 and the wild cluster bootstrap on nine clusters already takes the clustering from the data); rho is a planning input for the minimum detectable movement only.
4. **Wording (accepted).** Stage 1 has no null hypothesis; its 90% interval is a screening filter and not a significance claim. Direction is named in every report: slope below 1 is overconfident, above 1 underconfident.
5. **Logging fields (accepted, with additions).** One row per realised series per checkpoint: season, checkpoint week and games-played average, round, home-ice team, other team, higher-seed flag, q, outcome, po_scale in force. Test: run one season before and after the change and require identical futures CSVs (byte for byte), and q equal to a sampled series frequency within Monte Carlo error.
6. **Loose code-path check (reviewer's variant).** A completed season against an archived live snapshot would catch a gross divergence, but pipeline/sport_archive.py writes to ../archive outside this repository and none is available here; open until the archive is supplied.

## Amendment 2 (8 Nov 2026, reviewer round 10 checked; recorded before any logging or run; nothing above edited)
**Two preamble questions, required in this rule and proposed for every future rule.** (a) *If the model were perfect, would this return the same number?* If yes the test is void (the props opposite-side placebo and the two-sided shopping arm both failed this). (b) *If the intervention were switched on or off, would this statistic move at all?* If no the test cannot answer the question asked (the end-of-season series probability at po_scale 1, caught in Amendment 1, failed this, not (a): a perfect model there returns slope 1 and an imperfect one does not). The reviewer proposed (a) alone as covering all three; it does not cover the third.
1. **One gate companion (accepted).** The playoff-qualification slope at the same checkpoint and the same in-session baseline. Reasons: no series mechanics, and the most events (16 of 32 teams per season, about 144 over nine seasons) of any regular-season market. The reviewer's further reason (no points or OT dependence) is wrong: qualification is decided by points, so it carries the same OT sensitivity as division; that is accepted and stated. Division, round 2 (a compound), the points slope and the Cup are reported descriptively and cannot change the branch.
2. **Complete decision tree (the amended rule did not classify its own baseline).** Gate companion interval contains 1 and series interval excludes 1 -> series-channel defect, Stage 2 may be proposed. Gate companion excludes 1 and series excludes 1 in the same direction -> inherited from the ratings, Stage 2 is not authorised. Gate companion excludes 1 and series excludes 1 in opposite directions -> no authorisation, both recorded. Series interval contains 1 -> no series defect found (calibrated if the half-width is below the minimum detectable movement at rho 0.40, otherwise insufficient). Gate companion excludes 1 and series contains 1 -> ratings defect, series calibrated.
3. **Points slope (reviewer: attenuated by construction and contaminated by OT).** Checked on the nine-season live arm: preseason points slope 0.957 [0.78, 1.13], first quarter 1.047 [0.87, 1.21]. Not attenuated: for a calibrated shrinkage forecast the error is independent of the prediction (the Berkson case), so the slope is 1; the attenuation formula applies to a noisy measured predictor. The OT sensitivity is real but opposite in sign to the claim: on 2025 week 0 the OT fix compresses predicted points sd from 9.91 to 9.08 and raises the single-season slope from 0.316 to 0.348. The points slope stays descriptive, because it shares the OT dependence with the gate, not because it is attenuated.
4. **One variance treatment (accepted).** The season-clustered wild bootstrap is the operative inference. The rho band is a planning assumption for the minimum detectable movement only, and is not used for any pass or fail call. This supersedes the Amendment 1 sentence making an Insufficient call against the rho 0.40 figure: Insufficient is called when the bootstrap half-width exceeds the pre-computed minimum detectable movement quoted at rho 0.40.
5. **Byte-identical test.** Run on the densest season (2021, 28 weeks) and on 2025, before and after the logging change, plus the sampled-frequency check.
