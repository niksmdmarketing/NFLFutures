# Proposed amendments to NBA props price rule — 6 November 2026

Review of `claude/nba-props-price-test:research/NBA_PROPS_PRICE_TEST_RULE.md`, version 1. Proposals only: the NBA branch/rule/forecasts and collector are untouched. No price or outcome inspection informed these comments.

1. **Preserve the actual line on every update.** The current shared collector writes `handicap=meta.get('handicap')` and collapses history by book/market/outcome/player. That does not demonstrate that numeric line changes remain attached to updates. A metadata handicap flag or one market-level value cannot establish the line at entry and close. Require a verified line-aware reference export before this test runs. Do not score the compact export if distinct contracts were merged.
2. **Freeze one common entry line across books.** The rule chooses a main line per book but later takes a best price and consensus. Specify a common line by a deterministic rule before computing either. An over 20.5 probability cannot be combined with an under 21.5 probability or compared to a model forecast at a different line. Use each same-book/same-line pair for proportional de-vig, then average its probabilities.
3. **CLV must use the entry contract's line.** Query a closing over/under pair at the exact entry line, not whichever line is main at close. Freeze whether the consensus keeps the entry bookmaker set; report missing same-line closes and coverage. If the data cannot recover them, G2 cannot be evaluated. A price improvement compared with a different line is not CLV.
4. **Correct the final mechanics timing sentence.** Both the model and the observed entry quote must be available by the decision cutoff. The quote's `createdAt` is its last-change timestamp, so an unchanged quote can legitimately predate the forecast. Require forecast generation, and server receipt for forward snapshots, at/before cutoff; quote observation at/before cutoff; close strictly before kickoff. State source publication/observation provenance for reconstructed injury/lineup inputs and report forecast/quote ages.
5. **Make small-window G3 insufficient, not a pass.** The current wording says fewer than 50 bets does not fail, but leaves advancement ambiguous. Require both windows to be assessable and positive for an overall pass. An unassessable window blocks advancement and remains 'insufficient', without being called evidence against the model. Add a distinct-date count: 200 correlated player props over a few dates is not 200 independent tests.
6. **Freeze the regression and resampling details.** Include an intercept, exact clipping, penalty/no-penalty choice, standardization, convergence/separation policy and bootstrap interval method. Refit all coefficients inside each date-cluster draw. Keep all correlated player/game/line/book observations together. Report failed draws; do not silently discard them. Treat fitted-blend scores on window 1 as training scores; only the frozen blend applied to window 2 is prospective validation.
7. **Do not multiply observations through bookmaker duplication.** Specify one observation per common player/game/stat/line for G1, including all eligible non-bets. Select one side/book at most per player/game/stat for G2. Fix ties, quote-age limits, minimum consensus coverage and missing-close handling before outcomes. Matching denominator should be unique priced player-games, reported both overall and by book/stat/date window, with suffix collisions and ambiguous fixtures logged.
8. **Verify void and market scope rather than assume it.** Freeze each book's actual DNP, partial-participation, injury-void, overtime and abandoned-game rules. Confirm ESPN's appearance/stat labels correspond to that contract. Void/unknown participation is separate from under or zero. Keep the existing half-point restriction.
9. **Keep the conclusion narrow.** Positive CLV is evidence about prices relative to a reference close, not proof of profitable settlement. Report the market-only and model-only accuracy controls, unmodified thresholds, all three stats and multiple-comparison exposure. A historical pass qualifies the named stat for a forward paper test only, as the existing decision already says.

Suggested dated addition to the NBA rule: freeze these mechanics and the relevant data-schema corrections before the first scoring run. If a mechanics correction becomes necessary after seeing prices, disclose the exposure and preserve the original rule/results rather than describing the amendment as untouched preregistration.

Additional G1 clarification from review on 6 November: explicitly test identifiability. A penalized two-predictor regression can split a shared market signal across duplicate model/market logits, producing a positive model coefficient even without independent information. Require a full-rank, adequately conditioned design and an unpenalized identifiable fit; separation/non-convergence is insufficient, not a reason to choose a favorable penalty. An exact copy of market probabilities must never pass G1.
