## DraftKings

Detailed source: [DraftKings at Golden Nugget Lake Charles house rules](https://www.goldennugget.com/lake-charles/casino/draftkings-sportsbook-bar-grill/draftkings-sportsbook-house-rules/), version 18, dated `09.03.2026` as printed. This is Louisiana retail, not verified as the feed's jurisdiction.

Exact excerpts: basketball participation is “the player entering the Game according to the Game’s official box score.” Football participation means “playing in at least one play”. Football overtime: “All bets include overtime statistics.”

For ordinary full-game named-player markets, participation and the general/sport settlement rules govern; no appearance is not a zero-stat under. Brief appearance can establish participation, but a first-half injury/illness has a separate exception. The current detailed NBA and NFL rules void eligible full-game player-stat selections accepted before halftime if the player misses all of the second half including overtime due to injury/illness and team/league confirmation is released within two hours of game end. Unders and already determined selections are excluded. Thus an early injury can affect sides differently. Full-game NBA stats include overtime subject to specified unrelated market exceptions.

The [DraftKings Early Exit Protected support page](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/early-exit-program-early-exit-protected-overview?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0012036) says eligible selections “will be settled as Void”. The detailed house-rule exceptions must control interpretation. For receptions/pass attempts, zero statistics after confirmed participation would ordinarily mean zero absent another applicable exception; the exact total-market zero-stat clause and historical online rule versions remain unverified. Do not infer them from first-reception markets.

## FanDuel

Source: [New Jersey house rules](https://policies.fanduel.com/SB/NJ/2026-10-15T12-42-58.html), updated 15 October 2026, Part C basketball and American football.

Exact NBA excerpts: “All bets stand once a player takes the court, irrespective of game time played.” “All totals are inclusive of overtime.”

For ordinary NBA performance props, no participation voids; brief participation establishes action. For ordinary NFL player props the stated threshold is playing a snap; being active without any snap does not suffice. Zero full-game receptions or pass attempts after participation consequently settle at zero under the general participation rule; this is an inference, not a separate explicit zero-stat-total clause. First completion/reception markets have exceptions and are outside the requested full-game count totals. NFL overtime counts unless otherwise specified. A pregame ruled-out player who never participates is void under these thresholds. Do not extend live/drive/quarter-specific rules to pregame totals.

[FanDuel's injury support search result](https://fanduelgroup.my.site.com/hc/s/article/What-happens-to-my-prop-bet-if-a-player-is-inactive-or-injured) refers to Bet Protect/Bet Protect+ for certain NFL props; the retrieved page body was unreadable. Its eligibility, refund form and historical applicability are NOT established here and must not be encoded as a blanket injury void.

## Pinnacle

Sources: [international rules](https://www.pinnacle.com/en/future/betting-rules) (search-indexed clauses; direct retrieval blocked), [Canadian rules](https://www.pinnacle.ca/en/future/betting-rules) (readable, corroborating the relevant clauses). No historical effective date established.

Exact excerpt: “Passing Statistics: Bets will be deemed void if a named Quarterback doesn’t attempt at least one pass.”

NBA named-player statistics require playing; a nonparticipant is void, while brief participation meets that condition absent a market-specific exception. NBA full-game results include all overtimes. NFL likewise requires participation, with the stricter passing-stat attempt requirement. An active QB who plays but has zero attempts is therefore void for passing statistics. This is material for the pass-attempt test.

The receiving clause sets receiving yardage to zero when a named receiver plays without a catch. It does not explicitly name reception-count totals. A zero-receptions settlement cannot be claimed as an exact quoted rule from that yardage clause; it remains a specific verification gap. Full-game NFL includes overtime. A player ruled out after the quote and never participating is void under the participation condition.
