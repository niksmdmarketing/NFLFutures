Research cutoff: **27 October 2026, 15:53:55 AEDT (Sydney)** / 04:53:55 UTC. US articles retain their published local calendar dates. No forecasts or model settings were changed.

## Applying the rules

1. **Eligibility is a lower bound, not an expected absence.** IR was eight elapsed weeks in 2018–19, three team games under the temporary 2020–21 rules, and four team games from 2022. Start its clock at the placement transaction, not the injury. Count the actual team schedule. A bye does not satisfy a regular-season IR game requirement from 2020 onward.
2. The 2020 temporary rule superseded the earlier ordinary bylaw proposal. Do not use the proposal's eight-game/three-return rule for the actual 2020 season. Returns were unlimited at team level in 2020–21, with one per player; from 2022 there were eight combined IR/NFI designations and up to two per player. From 2024 playoff clubs get two additional postseason slots. Cutdown-day exceptions have separate eligibility conditions.
3. **Practice is not permission to play.** Designated to return normally opens a 21-day practice/activation window. A player can remain medically unavailable or fail to activate. In 2026 Reserve/PUP practice may begin after the team's second game, although the playing minimum remains four. The amendment explicitly names PUP; extending that new early-practice permission to NFI was not verified. [Approved 2026 change](https://www.nfl.com/playerhealthandsafety/resources/press-releases/nfl-announces-new-playing-rules-bylaws-resolutions-for-2026).
4. Initial-season PUP/NFI is not interchangeable with in-season IR/NFI. The older initial-season PUP minimum is six calendar weeks, including a bye. For the modern four-game threshold, official calendars contain wording that includes a bye, while club explanations say four games. An exceptional early-bye interpretation was **not found** conclusively; the CSV flags this instead of inventing a resolution. Initial-season NFI return-cap treatment was also not established for every year. [Current official calendar](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-important-dates).
5. “Week to week,” “day to day,” an Out designation or no IR placement does not supply an exact multi-game forecast. An absence reported in weeks requires dated calendar/schedule handling; do not substitute games. A report of a range must keep both endpoints. Concussion has no automatic fixed missed-game minimum.
6. Suspensions usually specify games; those byes do not count. But some notices specify weeks, dates, a season or an indefinite ban. Read the actual notice and appeal/reinstatement status. The table is not a complete archive of every annual disciplinary policy.
7. COVID reserve was not ordinary IR. Rules depended on date, testing, symptoms, vaccination status and positive-test versus close-contact status. The October 2020 protocol and August/December 2021 versions are identified. The older pre-October 2020 high-risk-contact wording was not fully verified. Do not turn every COVID placement into three missed games. Voluntary season opt-outs are separate.

## Current QB findings and limits

The located reports identify **Jackson, Dart and Hurts as unavailable at their most recent relevant game**, with Jackson's Week 8 status unresolved at the cutoff. Hurts' medical condition is included because this request covers all availability; it is not treated as a traumatic injury.

Located returns include Mayfield, Williams, Daniels, Murray, Penix and Darnold. A return removes an old forecast of absence; it does not prove that a player is unrestricted or cleared for every future game. McCarthy has replaced Winston as Dart's stand-in. Reports retain Cousins and Brissett in their starting roles; speculation about rookie replacements is not recorded as a confirmed switch.

**The availability CSV is a sourced research snapshot, not a certified complete transaction feed.** “Earliest” means the earliest report located here, not proof that no earlier public report exists. For several teams, the latest evidence is a recent appearance or preview rather than an explicit next-game medical clearance. Those limits remain in the rows. Dallas/Indianapolis have weaker current confirmation than teams with located postgame evidence. Full game-by-game replacement sequencing for Chicago was not established. No absence located is not the same as fully fit.
