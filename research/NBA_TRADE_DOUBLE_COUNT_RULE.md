# NBA: does the roster step double count traded players in futures? Rule written BEFORE any full run (9 Nov 2026)
Picked by TypeSafe (91%) as the next job under research/NEWS_IN_FUTURES_RULE.md. Designed and coded by DeepSeek (relay 028); Claude reviewed, corrected and runs it.
- Live: current roster = today's (includes mid-season trades); roster step coefficient 1.0 from game 20 on, never fades; ratings are results-weighted. After a trade the results increasingly contain the player at his new team while the roster step still adds his full value (and the old team is symmetrically over-penalised).
- Arms (research/replay_nba.py, live flags BT_FULL=1 BT_PO_K=1 BT_PO_GAMMA=1.5 BT_PO_LASTPO=1 BT_PO_GAMES=1, 1000 sims, 2013-2026, 2020 skipped = 13 seasons): N = opening-night roster all season (existing replay; does not see trades); A (BT_TRADES=A) = point-in-time roster, live behaviour, the REFERENCE; B (BT_TRADES=B) = A with each team's trade contribution (A roster step minus N roster step) weighted by: new team, share of its games so far without the player; old team, share of its games so far with him (several moves on one team: mean).
- Claude's corrections to DeepSeek's code: the roster step is not additive in players (12-man rotation, minute budget, clip at +/-6, demeaning), so B weights per-team differences rather than per-player pieces (DeepSeek's additivity assertion would fail); DeepSeek's old-team correction had the sign reversed.
- Gate 0: at week 0 A and B equal N exactly (checked on 2024: identical).
- Primary: paired B - A log loss, in-season only, equal weight over markets then seasons, season-cluster bootstrap. No-harm: B not adopted if any market is worse than A by more than 0.0005 or the 90% lower bound of the combined gain is below -0.0005. Adoption as a correctness fix needs the measured removed double count > 0.05 sd of team ratings in at least 3 team-seasons across 3 seasons, no-harm, and a non-negative point estimate. Family 1. EXPLORATORY (spent seasons): a pass authorises a shadow only.
- Falsification: if B is worse than A by more than N is worse than A, the double-count diagnosis is wrong; no adoption, question closed for history.
