v5: in-season betting number

Chances from the candidate model v5, with the live model v4 in grey. On 2013–2025, replayed with no hindsight, v5 was more accurate in season (after weeks 4, 9 and 13) for divisions and playoffs in 10 of 13 seasons, but the gain was not clear enough to pass its test, and its pre-season playoff chances were worse. Using v5 during the season is a judgement call (27 Oct 2026); v4 stays the main model for 2026. Both are saved and scored against Polymarket prices.
